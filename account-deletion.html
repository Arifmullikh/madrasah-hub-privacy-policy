Madrasah Hub Account & Data Deletion

Google Play 2026 Compliant Deletion Portal · AM Core Studios

Madrasah Hub accounts are provisioned for institutional staff (Muhtamims & Ustads) and linked Guardians. Use the options below to request permanent account removal or wipe local device data at any time.

01 Institutional Removal (Through Your Madrasah Office)

  1. Ustads: Your Muhtamim can immediately deactivate your access under More → Ustads → Deactivate and remove your Firebase Auth profile.
  2. Guardians / Students: Contact your Madrasah office to unlink your mobile number or archive/permanently delete the student profile inside the app.
  3. Local App Data: Clearing app data in Android Settings or uninstalling Madrasah Hub permanently erases all local Room caches, downloaded kitabs/audio, and on-device receipt photos.
