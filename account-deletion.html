Accounts in Jamia Islamia Darul Ulum Sheikhdi (জামিয়া ইসলামিয়া দারুল উলুম শেখদী) are provisioned for Muhtamims, Ustads, and linked Guardians. Use the options below to request permanent account removal or clear local app data anytime.

01 Institutional Removal (Through Madrasah Office)

  1. Ustads: The Muhtamim can immediately deactivate your access under More → Ustads → Deactivate and remove your Firebase Auth profile.
  2. Guardians / Students: Contact the Madrasah office to unlink your mobile number or archive/permanently delete the student profile inside the app.
  3. Local App Data: Clearing app data in Android Settings or uninstalling the app permanently erases all local Room caches, downloaded kitabs/audio, and on-device receipt photos.
