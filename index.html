01

Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Jamia Islamia Darul Ulum Sheikhdi (জামিয়া ইসলামিয়া দারুল উলুম শেখদী) (Android package com.amcorestudios.madrasa1), developed by AM Core Studios ("we", "our", or "us"), collects, stores, uses, and safeguards data when used by madrasah administrators (Muhtamims), teachers (Ustads), and guardians.

The application brings together institutional madrasah management (daily attendance, student enrollment, tuition and fee receipts, exam marksheets, notices, and staff leave) and offline-first Islamic learning tools (Quran, Tafsir, Hadith, Qawmi Kitab, Prayer Times, and Qibla).

Core Promise: Jamia Islamia Darul Ulum Sheikhdi contains no advertisements, no ad-network SDKs, and no behavioral tracking. We never sell, rent, or commercialize student, guardian, or staff records.
02

Accounts, Roles & Authentication

Access inside the app is strictly role-based and authenticated through Firebase Authentication:

  • Muhtamim / Admin: Signs in with email and password. Manages classes (jamats), students, ustad accounts, fees, exams, notices, settings, and local office backups.
  • Ustad (Teacher): Signs in with office-issued email and password. Server-enforced Cloud Firestore rules restrict access strictly to assigned jamats and specific permissions granted by the Muhtamim.
  • Guardian (Parent): Signs in with their registered Bangladesh mobile number and a 4–6 digit secret PIN (updatable anytime in Settings). Guardians receive read-only access strictly to their linked child's attendance, notices, receipts, and published results.

Passwords and guardian PINs are managed solely by Firebase Authentication and are never stored in plain text in Cloud Firestore or exported office backups.

03

Institutional & App Data We Process

Depending on your madrasah's configuration and your assigned role, the application processes:

  • Account & Profile Metadata: Name, role (admin, ustad, guardian), staff email, guardian phone number, assigned class IDs, linked student IDs, and active/inactive status.
  • Student & Academic Records: Student name, optional roll number, admission date, jamat assignment, monthly fee amount, guardian name, primary/alternate guardian phone numbers, WhatsApp flag, daily attendance (present, absent, late, leave), routines, exam subjects, and marks.
  • Tuition & Fee Ledgers: Tuition payment months, fee types, amounts, dates, payment method labels (Cash, bKash, Nagad, Rocket, Bank), receipt numbers, and optional notes recorded by staff.
  • Staff Leave, Notices & Audit Logs: Leave dates and reason categories, madrasah notices, message dispatch history, and staff activity logs for administrative accountability.
  • On-Device Worship & Reading State: Quran reading progress, bookmarks, ayah notes, hifz practice, tasbih counts, daily prayer tracker masks, display fonts/themes, and downloaded audio/kitab files stored locally via Android Room, SharedPreferences, and Jetpack DataStore.

05

Location, Qibla, Prayer Times & Mosques

Jamia Islamia Darul Ulum Sheikhdi never runs background location tracking, geofencing, or location history logging. A single foreground GPS/network fix is requested only when you tap to locate inside Qibla, Prayer Times, or Nearby Mosques.

  • Prayer Times (Aladhan API): Coordinates are rounded to approximately 100 meters (%.3f precision) along with your device timezone when querying api.aladhan.com, then cached locally per month.
  • Nearby Mosques (OpenStreetMap): Queries public OpenStreetMap Overpass API mirrors (overpass.private.coffee, maps.mail.ru, overpass-api.de) and caches nearby mosque results locally. Opening directions hands off to your chosen maps app (e.g., Google Maps).
06

Guardian Messaging, WhatsApp, SMS & Receipts

When staff send attendance alerts, dues reminders, notices, marksheet PDFs, or fee receipts:

  • User-Initiated Local Dispatch: In standard mode, the app checks whether WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business is installed and opens a pre-filled wa.me chat, native SMS composer, or Android Share Sheet one guardian at a time—strictly upon staff action.
  • Optional Server-Side Cloud Functions Backend: If the institution deploys the optional Firebase Cloud Functions + Neon PostgreSQL API, attendance/notice templates can be dispatched via Meta WhatsApp Cloud API, an institutional Bangladesh SMS gateway, or FCM push notifications using stored device tokens.
  • No Address Book Access: The app never reads or uploads your phone's personal contacts list; only guardian phone numbers explicitly entered into student records by madrasah staff are used.
07

Islamic Content, Media Streams & Third-Party Services

Core Quran Mushafs, translations, duas, and foundational Islamic guides are bundled offline inside the app. When you stream or download optional online resources, your device connects directly to:

  • Google Firebase: Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore (with local persistence), and Firebase Cloud Messaging for secure institutional login, data sync, and notifications.
  • Quran, Tafsir & Recitation Providers: api.quran.com, api.alquran.cloud, everyayah.com, and islamicapi.com (Ruqyah audio) when downloading recitations, tafsir, or word meanings.
  • Qawmi Kitab & Hadith Packs: Public content packs hosted via GitHub (Arifmullikh/annur-content, Arifmullikh/qawmi-kitab) for optional offline PDF/book downloads.
  • Live Makkah & Madinah Broadcasts: Saudi Broadcasting Authority (SBA) Quran TV and Sunnah TV HLS streams distributed via Globecast/Akamai CDN (cdn-globecast.akamaized.net).
08

Tuition & Fee Ledger Transparency

Important Clarification: Jamia Islamia Darul Ulum Sheikhdi provides a bookkeeping ledger and receipt generator for cash, bKash, Nagad, Rocket, and bank payments settled with the madrasah office. The app does not process live card transactions, mobile-banking PINs, OTPs, or payment gateway credentials.
09

Children's & Student Privacy

The app is built for adult administrators, ustads, and parents/guardians. Students do not create self-service public accounts, and the app contains zero advertisements, zero social feeds, and zero unmoderated chat.

Student enrollment, attendance, fee, and academic records are entered solely by authorized madrasah officials and are accessible strictly to authorized institutional staff and the linked guardian in accordance with applicable privacy standards.

10

Data Retention, Backups & Deletion Rights

You and your madrasah retain full control over stored records:

  • Institutional Record Management: Muhtamims can deactivate ustad accounts, archive or permanently delete student profiles, and export sanitized Excel (.xlsx) or JSON backups locally via Settings → Data Backup.
  • Local Device Wipe: Uninstalling the app or clearing Android App Storage permanently removes all cached local databases, preferences, downloaded books/audio, and on-device receipt photos.
  • Account & Cloud Data Deletion: To request removal of your Firebase Auth account or personal profile records, contact your Madrasah office or use our dedicated Account Deletion Portal. Requests sent to arifmullikh.personal@gmail.com are verified and processed within 48 hours (subject to institutional retention of required financial/audit ledgers where applicable).
  • Policy Updates: Material updates to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
