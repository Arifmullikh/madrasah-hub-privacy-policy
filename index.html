01

Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Madrasah Hub (মাদরাসা হাব) (Android package com.amcorestudios.madrasa1), developed by AM Core Studios ("we", "our", or "us"), collects, stores, uses, and protects information when used by madrasah administrators (Muhtamims), teachers (Ustads), and guardians.

Madrasah Hub combines institutional madrasah administration (attendance, student rosters, tuition and fee receipts, exam marksheets, notices, and staff leave) with offline-first Islamic worship and study tools (Quran, Tafsir, Hadith, Qawmi Kitab, Prayer Times, and Qibla).

Core Commitment: Madrasah Hub contains no advertisements, no ad-network SDKs, and no behavioral profiling. We never sell, rent, or trade student, guardian, or staff information to marketers or data brokers.
02

Accounts, Roles & Authentication

Access inside Madrasah Hub is strictly role-based and provisioned through Firebase Authentication:

  • Muhtamim / Admin: Authenticates via email and password. Has institutional oversight over classes (jamats), students, staff accounts, fees, exams, notices, settings, and local data exports.
  • Ustad (Teacher): Authenticates via office-assigned email and password. Access is scoped by server-enforced Firestore rules strictly to their assigned jamats and specific permissions granted by the Muhtamim (e.g., attendance, messaging, student addition, or fee collection).
  • Guardian (Parent): Authenticates using their registered Bangladesh mobile number and a 4–6 digit secret PIN (which they can change anytime inside Settings). Guardians have read-only access limited strictly to their linked child's attendance, fee receipts, notices, and published exam results.

Passwords and guardian PINs are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never stored in plain text inside Cloud Firestore or included in exported office backups.

03

Institutional & App Data We Process

Depending on your madrasah's configuration and your assigned role, the app processes the following categories of data:

  • Account & Profile Metadata: Display name, role (admin, ustad, guardian), staff email address, guardian phone number, assigned class IDs, linked student IDs, and active/inactive account status.
  • Student & Academic Records: Student name, optional roll number, admission date, jamat/class assignment, monthly tuition rate, guardian name, primary/alternate guardian phone numbers, WhatsApp availability flag, daily attendance records (present, absent, late, leave), class routines, exam subjects, and exam marks.
  • Tuition & Fee Collection Logs: Tuition payment months, fee types, amounts, payment dates, payment method labels (Cash, bKash, Nagad, Rocket, Bank), receipt numbers, and optional notes recorded by authorized staff.
  • Staff Leave, Notices & Audit Logs: Leave dates and reason categories (illness, family, emergency, travel, other), madrasah notices, message dispatch history, and staff activity logs for administrative accountability.
  • On-Device Worship & Reading Preferences: Quran reading progress, bookmarks, ayah notes, hifz practice state, tasbih counts, daily prayer tracker masks, typography/theme preferences, and downloaded audio/kitab files — stored locally via Android Room, SharedPreferences, and Jetpack DataStore.

05

Location, Qibla, Prayer Times & Mosques

Madrasah Hub never runs background location tracking, geofencing, or location history logging. A single foreground GPS/network fix is requested only when you tap to locate inside Qibla, Prayer Times, or Nearby Mosques.

  • Prayer Times (Aladhan API): Coordinates are rounded to approximately 100 meters (%.3f precision) along with your device timezone when querying api.aladhan.com, then cached on-device per month.
  • Nearby Mosques (OpenStreetMap): Queries public OpenStreetMap Overpass API mirrors (overpass.private.coffee, maps.mail.ru, overpass-api.de) and caches nearby mosque results locally. Opening directions hands off to your chosen maps app (e.g., Google Maps).
06

Guardian Messaging, WhatsApp, SMS & Receipts

When staff send attendance alerts, dues reminders, notices, marksheet PDFs, or fee receipts:

  • User-Initiated Local Dispatch: In standard mode, Madrasah Hub queries whether WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business is installed and opens a pre-filled wa.me chat, native SMS composer, or Android Share Sheet one guardian at a time—strictly upon staff action.
  • Optional Server-Side Cloud Functions Backend: If an institution deploys the optional Firebase Cloud Functions + Neon PostgreSQL API, attendance/notice templates can be dispatched via Meta WhatsApp Cloud API, an institutional Bangladesh SMS gateway, or FCM push notifications using stored device tokens.
  • No Contact List Harvesting: The app never accesses or uploads your phone's address book or contacts list; only guardian phone numbers explicitly entered into student records by madrasah staff are used.
07

Islamic Content, Media Streams & Third-Party Services

Core Quran Mushafs, translations, duas, and foundational Islamic guides are bundled offline inside the app. When you stream or download optional online resources, your device connects directly to:

  • Google Firebase: Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore (with local persistence), and Firebase Cloud Messaging for secure institutional login, data sync, and notifications.
  • Quran, Tafsir & Recitation Providers: api.quran.com, api.alquran.cloud, everyayah.com, and islamicapi.com (Ruqyah audio) when downloading recitations, tafsir, or word meanings.
  • Qawmi Kitab & Hadith Packs: Public content packs hosted via GitHub (Arifmullikh/annur-content, Arifmullikh/qawmi-kitab) for optional offline PDF/book downloads.
  • Live Makkah & Madinah Broadcasts: Saudi Broadcasting Authority (SBA) Quran TV and Sunnah TV HLS streams distributed via Globecast/Akamai CDN (cdn-globecast.akamaized.net).
08

Tuition & Fee Ledger Transparency

Important Clarification: Madrasah Hub provides a bookkeeping ledger and receipt generator for cash, bKash, Nagad, Rocket, and bank payments already settled with the madrasah office. The app does not process live card transactions, mobile-banking PINs, OTPs, or payment gateway credentials.
09

Children's & Student Privacy

Madrasah Hub is built for adult administrators, ustads, and parents/guardians. Students do not create self-service public accounts, and the app contains zero advertisements, zero social feeds, and zero unmoderated chat.

Student enrollment, attendance, fee, and academic records are entered solely by authorized madrasah officials and are accessible strictly to authorized institutional staff and the linked guardian in accordance with applicable privacy standards.

10

Data Retention, Backups & Deletion Rights

You and your madrasah retain control over stored records:

  • Institutional Record Management: Muhtamims can deactivate ustad accounts, archive or permanently delete student profiles, and export sanitized Excel (.xlsx) or JSON backups locally via Settings → Data Backup.
  • Local Device Wipe: Uninstalling Madrasah Hub or clearing Android App Storage permanently removes all cached local databases, preferences, downloaded books/audio, and on-device receipt photos.
  • Account & Cloud Data Deletion: To request removal of your Firebase Auth account or personal profile records, contact your Madrasah office or use our dedicated Account Deletion Portal. Requests sent to arifmullikh.personal@gmail.com are verified and processed within 48 hours (subject to institutional retention of required financial/audit ledgers where applicable).
  • Policy Updates: Material updates to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
